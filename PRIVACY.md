# Privacy Notice

Effective September 24, 2026

Spanish Vocab Widget runs locally on your Mac. The app does not create an
account, display advertising, collect analytics, or upload your vocabulary or
usage data.

The app stores its settings in macOS user defaults and stores the active
vocabulary CSV in your Documents folder. Speech is produced using voices
installed on your Mac.

When you select Update, the app contacts GitHub to check the project's latest
release and may download an update. When you select Pay with PayPal, your web
browser opens the payment page. GitHub and PayPal process those interactions
under their own privacy policies.

Payment details are handled by PayPal. The app does not receive or store your
payment-card or PayPal credentials.

Privacy questions may be sent to spanishvocab@onda.aleeas.com.
